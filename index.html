01 ABOUT ME

Engineering rigor.
Intelligent solutions.

I work at the intersection of electric power systems, artificial intelligence, and mathematical optimization. My research asks a practical question: how can learned models make decisions quickly without losing sight of the physics and uncertainty that matter in the real world?

As a PhD researcher at the University of Mississippi, I investigate graph-based learning for power-system operation, with an emphasis on reliability, physical feasibility, and decision-making under uncertain renewable generation.

My perspective also draws on experience in the energy industry, spanning electricity distribution and renewable-energy development. I care about methods that are not only technically sophisticated, but useful beyond the lab.
